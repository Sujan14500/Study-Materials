How to actually use these

  • Do the interactive bits. Every course is built around widgets you can push until they break. Reading past them is the fastest way to feel productive and learn nothing.
  • Start the interview bank on day one. Not at the end. Use Study mode to check whether a chapter landed, and let the flashcards space it out for you.
  • Say answers out loud. The gap between recognising an answer and producing one under a clock is enormous, and it is the gap interviews measure.
  • Build one thing. A RAG system over documents you actually care about teaches more than three courses, and it gives you something to talk about.
  • Come back to Stage 0. If evaluation or vector maths feels slippery later, the fundamentals courses are where that gets fixed — not by re-reading the advanced chapter.
