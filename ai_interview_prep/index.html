Study mode

Every question, answered twice

Once in plain English so it sticks, once at interview depth so it survives a follow-up. Filter by topic and level, search anything, and mark what you already know so the flashcards and mock interview stop showing it to you.

Flashcards

Recall, not recognition

Reading an answer feels like learning and is not. Say the answer out loud first, then flip. Cards you rate badly come back sooner — the schedule is stored in your browser, so a card you nailed today will not waste your time tomorrow.

Quiz

Multiple choice, no mercy

Every wrong option here is a real misconception someone said in a real interview. The explanation after each question tells you why the tempting answer is wrong, which is the part worth remembering.

Mock interview

A real round, on the clock

Questions arrive one at a time with a timer. Answer out loud before you reveal anything — the gap between "I know this" and "I can say this in ninety seconds" is the entire reason people fail interviews they were qualified for.
